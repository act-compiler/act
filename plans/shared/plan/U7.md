# U7 Conformance corpus with expected verdicts

- What: the corpus directory, its case format, and its runner belong to T5 and T6; this item adds only the merge of the mini_trn copies and the generator CI.
- What: the three mini_trn copies (taidl/examples/mini_trn.py, act-oracle/examples/mini_trn/mini_trn.py, act-backend/examples/mini_trn/mini_trn.py:99) become one entry.
- What: each generator's CI runs the corpus at the pinned front-end tag.
- What: the entries are the converted files of U13 once they exist; the equivalence test of U13 reads the same directory.
- What: a verdict is accepted, rejected by `check`, or per generator accepted, excluded (the instruction named, with its code), or rejected, as T5 defines it.
- Benefits: divergence such as X3 to X6 shows as a failed verdict, not a user report.
- Caveats: verdicts per generator must name the unsupported-feature row of L7, or the corpus encodes accidents; an exclusion verdict names the instruction and the code of its reason.
- Fail points: a generator updating its expected verdicts to match a bug; noticed by review of any change to a verdict file.
- Order and dependencies: after U3 and T5; with U4, U5, L8, T6.
- Ownership: T11 owns the one `coverage.md` renderer (registry for text, expectations for verdicts); the corpus supplies expectations and does not render.
- Verification: every X bug has an entry that fails before its fix and passes after.
