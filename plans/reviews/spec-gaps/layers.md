# Spec gaps: layers, encodings, derived attributes, generator metadata, the generator contract

Slice: layers.md (S11), generator_metadata.md (S12), object_model.md (S4.6), checked against shared plan U2, U3, U10, U11, panel 10, and conformance.md (S9).
All paths below are under plans/language/spec-draft/ unless they start with plans/ or shared/.
"Debate" means a real design choice; "no" means a clarification that follows the panel text.
Names in proposed text that the spec does not yet fix (`contract_version`, `is_hbm`, `metadata_namespaces`) are placeholders to be settled with U2.

## Verdict

- The three layers are mostly closed, but the one worked example of the encoding layer is rejected by its own rule (LY-01).
- The encode and decode sentence is wrong for half of the legal links (LY-02), and the accepted link spellings are undefined (LY-03).
- S9 lists exactly what is rejected, so every malformed `acc.encode` argument not listed is accepted with no meaning (LY-07).
- S4.6 omits every layer member that U2 promises, so the assembler generator has nothing in the spec to read (LY-18, LY-19).
- S12 leaves the entry-point result, the output directory, the version check, and the code scope of an outside generator open (LY-30 to LY-34).
