# Spec gaps: expressions, semantics, operations, numerics, faults

Slice: expressions.md (S6), semantics.md (S7), operations.md and operation_meaning.md (S8), and the S1.3 table in overview.md.
Read against the draft as of 2026-11-05 09:55 UTC; the pages were being edited during the review, so line numbers follow that snapshot.
Each row gives a gap, the exact text to add or replace, and whether the fix is a real design choice (a debate) or a clarification.
