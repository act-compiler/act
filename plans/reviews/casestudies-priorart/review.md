# Review: case studies and prior art

Reviewer role: accelerator architect.
Targets: `casestudies/` (ten sketches, `gaps.md`, `strengths.md`, C1 to C13) and `priorart/` (`borrow.md`, per-system files, P1 to P12).
Spot checks: Intel AMX against the SDM text at https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/ldtilecfg and https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/tileloadd:tileloaddt1; Arm SME against https://developer.arm.com/community/arm-community-blogs/b/architectures-and-processors-blog/posts/arm-scalable-matrix-extension-introduction; NVIDIA Hopper against the PTX ISA index at https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/parallel-thread-execution/index.html and the shape table at https://docs.rs/crate/oxicuda-ptx/0.1.8/source/src/tensor_core/wgmma.rs (secondary).

## Verdict

The ten sketches are faithful where they claim fidelity: the AMX VNNI pair layout, the zeroing of unused tile rows, the SME every-fourth-row tile mapping, the Hopper 128-byte swizzle, the 228 KiB shared memory, and the 32 accumulator registers per thread all match the public documents.
The verdict tables are mostly right, but three verdicts are wrong in the direction that inflates the gap matrix: `ldtilecfg` is expressible as is, indirect DMA is expressible with a workaround through `gather`, and `smstop` is a defined fault rather than an undefined value.
The gap matrix is ranked sensibly by count, but rank 1 rests on an implementation-defined byte order that owner decision 16 removes anyway, and two rows count invented command sets (Eyeriss, CGRA) as evidence.
Five of the nine language extensions C1 to C9 duplicate rows of `language/proposals.md` under different syntax, which is the main structural defect of the track.
R59 cuts bit operators, indexed state, array constants, VLIW bundles, and random state if the count is taken from `gaps.md`; a recount against descriptors and barriers gives bit operators and indexed state a second site, so the cut is not final until that recount is written down.
No major accelerator class is missing from the matrix, but the descriptor-driven engine with no instruction stream (Gaudi MME, NVDLA, Apple ANE, MTIA fixed-function units) is represented only by the invented Eyeriss command set, and the Ascend study alone does not fill that hole.
The prior-art borrowings are worth their cost for the spec (P1, P2 after one fix, P7) and are backend refactors with a borrowed name for the rest (P3, P4, P6, P10, P12), where the value depends on the B track and not on prior art.
No blocker; the track can proceed once the majors below are folded into the plan files.
