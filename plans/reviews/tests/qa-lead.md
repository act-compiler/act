# QA lead review: test suite plan

Review of `plans/tests/` against owner decisions 15 to 17 and rulings R3 to R12, R43 to R49, R55 to R63.
Code claims cite the read-only trees under `/home/user`; plan claims cite the plan file and line.

## Verdict

Not ready to approve: two blockers and twelve majors stand between the plan and a corpus that makes "the generators follow the spec" checkable.
The front-end half is sound: one tagged corpus in taidl, a verdict runner, clause ids in messages, and expectation tables that render coverage.md.
The run-time half is not: the case format carries run behaviour only as `kernel.hlo`, which cannot express the instruction-level faults and aliasing that owner decisions 16 and 17 define.
Migration safety holds at the step level but breaks in three places, where a test goes before its replacement exists or before it runs at the same cadence.
The missing policies (front-end fuzzing, mutation testing, CI time budget, flaky tests, golden updates) are cheap to add and should be added before Q1 starts.

## What the plan does well

- One corpus in taidl, shipped in the wheel and read through the pin, gives the language and its tests one version (T16, T19).
- Every rejection cites an S9 clause id, which makes the front-end verdict checkable by id rather than by prose (T6, R44).
- The expectation table renders coverage.md, and a byte-compare in CI makes the generator docs impossible to drift (T11, T21).
- A corpus case with no expectation row fails collection, so an unsupported feature is documented on the day it is tested (generators.md:21).
- The bug list is concrete and mostly accurate: TB1 to TB3, TB12, and TB14 to TB17 were confirmed against the code.
- The migration deletes each replaced test in the same PR as its replacement and keeps CLI, packaging, and `impl` tests under a stated rule (T20).
- T8 forbids a reference computed by the library the oracle lowers to, which closes the "both wrong together" hole.
- The golden record with provenance and the reference-before-golden rule are kept (`act-backend/tests/regenerate.py:35-52`).
