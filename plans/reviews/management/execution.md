# Management review: execution (engineering director)

Lens: execution risk, sequencing, hidden dependencies, migration safety, what engineers working with Claude trip on first, maintainability.
Read: plans/README.md, roadmap/ (waves, milestones, risks, load), minutes/owner-decisions.md, reviews/approval.md, meeting/panel-12/decision.md, and the language, shared, act, tests, backend, oracle plan files cited below.
Method: the wave tables were parsed read-only to recompute the longest chain and the ancestors of U8 and A23.

## Verdict

Executable with conditions.
The skeleton is sound: gates close in wave 1, the Q11.4 order sequences check, generators, tracer, converter, deletion, and the release order is explicit (waves.md:47-58).
The risks are in what the graph does not enforce.
The release can be cut by the graph without most M4 evidence, the biggest new feature sits last on the critical path, one wave 4 item depends on a release step it does not list, and four pairs of items describe one deliverable under two IDs.
The roadmap also lags the plans: L46 is missing, panel 12 is not yet in the waves, and the capacity tripwire uses a stale total.
Against the goals: robustness and migration safety are weakest (E1, E3, E14), maintainability is at risk from duplicated items and duplicated shape rules (E4, E8), retargetability and reusability hold.
