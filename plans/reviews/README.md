# Reviews

Independent reviews of the track plans, each written without the track's own reasoning in hand.
The tech-lead rulings take each review into account.

- [backend/](backend/README.md): reviews of the act-backend plan
- [oracle/](oracle/README.md): reviews of the act-oracle plan
- [spec/](spec/README.md): reviews of the draft language specification
- [tests/](tests/README.md): reviews of the test plan
- [semantics/](semantics/README.md): reviews of the formal core
- [shared-diagnostics/](shared-diagnostics/README.md): review of the shared-code and diagnostics plans
- [casestudies-priorart/](casestudies-priorart/README.md): review of the case studies and prior art
- [act-glossary/](act-glossary/README.md): review of the act plan, glossary, and terminology
- [rulings/](rulings/README.md): audit of the tech-lead rulings
- [issue-log/](issue-log/README.md): every review finding with severity, disposition, and where it landed, per review group
- [approval.md](approval.md): final approval of the plans, with the fixes made and the conditions in force
