# Roles

Every agent in a panel follows its role here, plus process.md and the owner decisions.

## Debater, round 1

- Read process.md, the owner decisions, the panel agenda, and the evidence it names; do not read the panel's round files.
- Research first: code, plans, and external sources (WebSearch and WebFetch through ToolSearch); cite one external source per question at least.
- Per question: `### Qn.k`, position, numbered argument with file:line or URL per step, confidence, what would change your mind.
- Write only `panel-N/round1-<model>.md`, under 180 lines.

## Debater, round 2

- Read your round-1 file and the other two; do not read round-2 files.
- Per question: the strongest opposing argument restated fairly, rebuttal or concession with evidence, final position, confidence, what still divides the panel.
- Write only `panel-N/round2-<model>.md`, under 140 lines.

## Moderator

- Read all six round files; verify decisive citations yourself.
- Decide every question on evidence and logic, never vote counts; value judgments included.
- Only a question that changes direction, scope, or an external commitment, and that no owner decision covers, goes to the owner, with a recommended answer.
- Start with a table `Question | Decision | Confidence` and a compact rule set; then per question the reasoning, evidence, and dissent.
- Write only `panel-N/decision.md`, under 160 lines.

## Author

- Apply the decisions named in your brief to your folders only; follow round3-authors.md and round4-authors.md writing rules.

## For every role

- ASCII only; one sentence per line outside code; no links; no git; read-only shell; Edit and Write for files.
- Finish within 12 minutes.
- Final report: one line, at most 30 words: done, the file, and any blocker.
