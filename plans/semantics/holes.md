## Ruling conventions

- "Fault" means every executor (oracle, simulator) stops the program at the issue with an error naming the instruction and the failed REQUIRE, and writes no region and no state; every producer (backend) never emits such an issue.
- "Reject" means the front end refuses the ISA specification, or a generator refuses an input with a coded error.
- "Define" removes the row from S1.3's undefined or implementation-defined table and adds the rule to the section cited.
- A ruling that changes a generator's current output is a breaking change for the coordinated major release.
