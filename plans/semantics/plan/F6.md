# F6 Byte order, sub-byte packing, and the size of d0

## What

- Spec S4.3 and S8.4: little-endian element layout in d0, low bits first for 2- and 4-bit types, and `|d0|` as a declared issue-time parameter.
- Verify act-oracle's `spec_bytes`, `packed`, and `sh_cast` against the rule; fix any mismatch.
- act-backend: expose `|d0|` as the allocator's HBM capacity.

## Benefits

- Closes H14 and the d0 part of H8; removes two implementation-defined rows from S1.3.

## Caveats

- A future big-endian target would need a declared byte order; add it only at the second target.

## Fail points

- The oracle's bitcast through `u8` for `pred` (utils.py:536-548) may differ from a packed bool on hardware; a test pins it.

## Order and dependencies

- Depends on L (S4.3, S8.4).
- Feeds O, B, T.

## Verification

- Round-trip tests: `store_rm` then `load_rm` on mini_trn preserves bf16 bits; a u4 kernel input fills whole bytes low bits first.
